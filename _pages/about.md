Hi, I'm Seongboo Park, a PhD student in Physics at the University of Ulsan.

My research is in computational materials science. I use density functional theory (DFT) and machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIP) to study materials.

## Research interests

- Ferroelectric domain walls: electronic structure and defect physics (HfO₂)
- Shift current and bulk photovoltaic effect in two-dimensional in-plane ferroelectrics
- Machine-learning interatomic potentials
- Phase-field simulation of ferroelectrics and multiscale modeling (DFT, MD, phase-field)
- High-throughput first-principles workflows
